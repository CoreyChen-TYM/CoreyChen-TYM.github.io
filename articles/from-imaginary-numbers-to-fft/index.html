From Imaginary Numbers to FFT

Overview · Part 1 · Part 2 · Part 3

Before the series begins

I started this series for two reasons. First, I wanted to put together the quickest path to learning the Fast Fourier Transform, so that those of you who also work on digital signal processing software can easily revisit the basic ideas of DSP.

Second, I wanted to revisit some of the more abstract concepts in textbooks and give them a clearer physical picture. This material brings together what I have learned from several books I have read.

I am planning five parts:

  1. Imaginary numbers and Euler’s formula

    A look back at how imaginary numbers were introduced, the history of Euler’s formula, and where the definition of the complex exponential function comes from.

  2. Why complex exponentials matter

    The key role complex exponentials play in Signals and Systems.

  3. The discrete Fourier series

    The Fourier series of discrete-time periodic signals (DFS).

  4. From DFS to DFT Planned

    Using DFS to derive the Discrete Fourier Transform.

  5. The Fast Fourier Transform Planned

    FFT is a fast algorithm for computing the DFT.

I hope to make these posts easy to follow without losing their depth—small, but thoughtfully put together.
