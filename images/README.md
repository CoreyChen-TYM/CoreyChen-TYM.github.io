## Vehicle photos

Journal project images are stored in `vehicles/`. Their source pages, original
image URLs, model notes, and dimensions are recorded in `vehicles.json`.
Images are downloaded unmodified from manufacturer websites and automotive
press galleries. They remain the property of their respective rights holders.
The years in that manifest are approximate years of Corey's involvement,
not vehicle launch dates. Some images represent a later iteration of an
ongoing project.
