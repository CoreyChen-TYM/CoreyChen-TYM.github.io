# Vehicle image cleanup

The built-in ImageGen tool was used to remove bottom-right overlays from:

- vehicles/xiaomi-su7.jpg: translucent PCAUTO media watermark.
- vehicles/aito-m7.jpg: translucent PCAUTO media watermark.
- vehicles/aion-y-plus.jpg: small white sales disclaimer, at the user’s request.

Prompt for SU7 and M7: Remove only the small translucent media watermark at
the extreme bottom right; reconstruct the studio floor/background; preserve
the vehicle, details, framing, colors, and lighting. Do not crop or redesign.

Prompt for AION Y Plus: Remove only the small white bottom-right Chinese text
and reconstruct the pavement. Keep the yellow vehicle, architecture, sky,
lighting, wide framing, and upper-left AION / AION Y Plus titles unchanged.

Generated outputs are encoded as JPEG at the existing asset dimensions.
Hover thumbnails are regenerated by scripts/generate_vehicle_previews.py.
Original source URLs remain in vehicles.json; original files are also available
in Git history. Local working backups are outside tracked website content.
